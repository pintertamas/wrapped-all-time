How to get your Spotify data export

Spotify offers two exports. Both are free, and both are read only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Which export do you need?

  • Account Data arrives within a few days and covers about the last 12 months of plays. Good for a quick look.
  • Extended streaming history covers your whole account lifetime, every year, and can take up to 30 days to arrive. Choose this one for all-time stats.

Request Account Data

  1. Open the Spotify website and sign in, then go to Account and then Privacy settings.
  2. Scroll to Download your data.
  3. Tick Account data and press Request data.
  4. Confirm the request in the email Spotify sends you.
  5. Wait for the second email, usually within a few days, and download the ZIP.

Request Extended streaming history

  1. On the same Privacy settings page, under Download your data, tick Extended streaming history.
  2. Press Request data and confirm in the email.
  3. Wait for the download email. This can take up to 30 days.
  4. Download the ZIP before the link expires.

Which file do I have?

Pick a file or ZIP to see which package it is. Only its name is read, on your device.
